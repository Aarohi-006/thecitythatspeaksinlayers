STICKER 06 · PRETZEL

ABOUT

Every sticker on a New York lamppost is someone saying I was here. This project collects those voices before the next layer covers them.

THE PROJECT

The City That Speaks in Layers started as a hand-bound book about urban stickers in New York City. This website turns it into something you can touch: instead of turning pages, you peel stickers. Each one opens a different layer of the city.
