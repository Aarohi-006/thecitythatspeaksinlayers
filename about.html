Before this project, a pole was a pole. Then I started looking closer, and the city turned out to be talking, in thousands of small, sticky voices, one layer on top of another.

THE PROJECT

The City That Speaks in Layers is a study of street stickers in New York City: who makes them, where they end up, and what happens to them once the city takes over. It began as a hand-bound book. This website turns the book into something you can touch. Instead of turning pages, you peel stickers, and every sticker opens a different layer.

WHY STICKERS

A sticker is the smallest way to speak in public. It costs almost nothing, it takes a second to put up, and anyone can make one. On a single pole you can find an artist's name, a band, a protest, an ad and a love note, all pressed together. Rain fades them, people peel them, new ones cover old ones. Nothing sticks forever, but something always remains.

HOW IT BEGAN: CITY COLLECTION

This website grew out of City Collection, an earlier project where I walked New York City with a camera and took 50 photographs of stickers on poles, doorways, mailboxes, bins and walls. From those photographs I wrote three stories, which became the three chapters of the book: Stick It to the City, No Space Is Silent and Nothing Sticks Forever. Walking became a way of collecting, and looking became a way of reading.

The drawings on the sticker sheet are my own: small New York icons, made to be peeled.
