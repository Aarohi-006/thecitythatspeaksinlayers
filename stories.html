THE BARE POLE

Under every layer, the same message: I was here.

LAYER 01 · CLAIMED

Snoe Man was here.

Pink, green and yellow SNOE MAN stickers march across the top of this doorway, again and again. Repeating your name is how you claim a spot. Underneath, every inch belongs to someone.

LAYER 02 · SCAN ME

Stickers that sell.

QR codes, @handles and "Download our app." A heart that says Love Is a Risk, Do It Anyway sits between ads and artist slaps. Here the sticker has become a business card.

LAYER 03 · TORN

Everyone at once.

A newspaper box with no newspapers. A poster ripped into white strips, a protest sticker, guitar lessons with a phone number, tags in white marker. Art, ads and politics on the same box.

LAYER 04 · SCRAPED

One survivor.

Someone scraped this wall clean: shredded paper, old paint, yellow spray lines underneath. In the middle, one tiny blue car sticker holds on. The smallest voice lasted the longest.
